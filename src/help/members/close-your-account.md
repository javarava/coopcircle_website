---
title: "Close your account"
description: "Ask to leave CoopCircle. Your societies first pay you your money and clear anything still open."
category: "your-account"
order: 4
app: "Coop Member"
---
1. Go to **Profile → Danger zone → Close account**.
2. For each society, you see your money (savings and funds to be paid to you, and shares), and anything that must be cleared first, such as a loan or a guarantee.
3. Choose a reason and confirm.

Your societies pay you your money before your account closes. You can download your data first: see [Download your data](/help/members/download-your-data/).
