---
title: "Download your data"
description: "Download everything your society keeps about you, at any time."
category: "your-account"
order: 3
app: "Coop Member"
---
1. Go to **Profile → Download my data**.
2. CoopCircle makes a download. It has your statement for the whole time you have been a member, and a file with your details and balances.
3. Choose **Download** when it’s ready.

If you join another society on CoopCircle, they can use this file to fill in your details.
