1. Go to **Loans** and choose **Apply for a loan**.
2. Choose a loan type. You see how much you can borrow, or why you can’t apply yet.
3. Enter the **amount** and the **loan period** (how long you will take to repay). Check the interest, each instalment and the total.
4. Say what the loan is for.
5. Name your **guarantors**, and how much each one guarantees. Add the documents the loan needs.
6. Send it, and confirm with your PIN.

Your guarantors are asked in Coop Member. If your society needs something more, the application is **sent back for changes** with a note. Change it and send it again. You can withdraw the application until a decision is made.
