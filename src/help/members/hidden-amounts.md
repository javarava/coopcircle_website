---
title: "Why are my amounts hidden?"
description: "Amounts stay hidden until you enter your PIN, so nobody looking at your phone sees them."
category: "pin"
order: 2
app: "Coop Member"
---
Coop Member hides your balances when it opens, when you leave the app, and when you sign out.

1. Tap the **eye** on your balance.
2. Enter your PIN.

Your amounts show for a few minutes, then hide again. You don’t need your PIN to hide them. Without internet, your PIN can’t be checked, so amounts stay hidden.
