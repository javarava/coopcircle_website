---
title: "Belong to more than one society"
description: "Use one account for every society you belong to, and switch between them."
category: "getting-started"
order: 3
app: "Coop Member"
---
If you are a member of more than one society on CoopCircle, you use one account for all of them.

- Coop Member opens the society you used last.
- To change, go to **Profile → Switch cooperative** and choose another.
- New invitations from another society appear in **Profile → Invitations**.

Each society can only see its own records.
