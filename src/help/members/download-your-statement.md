---
title: "Download your statement"
description: "Download a statement of your savings, funds, shares and loans for any dates, as a PDF."
category: "accounts"
order: 3
plan: "When your society is on Starter or above"
app: "Coop Member"
---
1. Go to **Accounts** and choose **Statement**.
2. Choose the dates.
3. Choose **View statement**, then **Share or save** or **Print**.

It is the same statement your society’s staff can print for you. If you don’t see **Statement**, your society’s plan doesn’t include it. Ask your branch for a printed copy.
