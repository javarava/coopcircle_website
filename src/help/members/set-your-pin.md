You enter your PIN when you ask to withdraw, apply for a loan, sign as a guarantor, change your bank account, close your account, or show hidden amounts.

1. Go to **Profile → Account PIN**.
2. Enter your account password.
3. Choose a 4-digit PIN and enter it twice. Easy PINs like 1234 aren’t allowed.

If you haven’t set one yet, you are asked to the first time you need it.

Never share your PIN, even with your society’s staff. They will never ask for it.
