Coop Member tells you when a payment arrives, when a withdrawal or loan moves forward, when someone asks you to be a guarantor, and when your society posts a notice for you.

- See them all with the **bell** on Home.
- Choose what you want to be told about in **Profile**, under notifications. You can make notifications silent.
- In a web browser on a computer or phone, choose **Allow** when it asks about notifications.
