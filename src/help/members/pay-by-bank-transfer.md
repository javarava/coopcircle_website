description: "Send a transfer from your banking app. Then tell your society, with the reference or a screenshot."

app: "Coop Member"
---
1. Choose the pay button on what you are paying for, then **Bank transfer**.
2. Copy your society’s account number and send the amount from your banking app. There is no fee.
3. Back in Coop Member, choose **I’ve sent the money**.
4. Add the **transfer reference**, a **screenshot** of the transfer, or both, and the day you sent it.
5. Choose **Send for checking**.

Your society checks its bank statement, then adds the payment to your account. You are told when it is added, or why not.

If you made a mistake, you can choose **Take back** until it is checked. You can have up to 5 transfers waiting to be checked, sent in the last 30 days.
