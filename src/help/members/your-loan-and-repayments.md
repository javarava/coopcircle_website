---
title: "Follow your loan and repayments"
description: "See your loan’s repayment dates, what you have paid and what is left."
category: "loans"
order: 3
app: "Coop Member"
---
Open the loan from **Loans** to see:

- where it is, from application, to paid to you, to repaid;
- the **schedule**: every instalment, its due date, and if it is paid;
- the **payments** you have made, each with its details;
- what is left to pay.

Repay at your branch. Or use **Make payment** on the loan, if your society takes payments in the app.
