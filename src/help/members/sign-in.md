---
title: "Sign in to Coop Member"
description: "Sign in with your email or phone number and your password."
category: "getting-started"
order: 2
app: "Coop Member"
---
1. Go to [member.coopcirclehq.com](https://member.coopcirclehq.com/sign-in) and choose **Sign in**.
2. Choose **Email** or **Phone**. You can sign in with your phone after you confirm it in **Profile → Phone number**.
3. Enter your password.

Forgot it? See [Reset your password or PIN](/help/members/forgot-pin-or-password/).
