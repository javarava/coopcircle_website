---
title: "See your savings, dues, loans and shares"
description: "See all your money in the society, and what you owe, in the Accounts and Loans tabs."
category: "accounts"
order: 1
app: "Coop Member"
---
- **Home** shows your savings, what’s due next, anything waiting for you (like a guarantor request) and recent activity.
- **Accounts** shows each savings account, your funds, the dues you owe and have paid, shares, insurance cover and dividends. Tap one to see every payment in and out.
- **Loans** shows your loans and applications, with each loan’s repayment dates and payments.

Payments appear as soon as your society records them. If something looks wrong, ask your branch.
