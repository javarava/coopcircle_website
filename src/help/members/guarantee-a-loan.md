title: "Guarantee a loan"
description: "When another member asks you to be a guarantor for their loan, read it, then agree or say no."
category: "loans"
order: 2
app: "Coop Member"
---
Guarantor requests appear first on **Home**, and you get a notification.

1. Choose **Review and sign**.
2. Read about the loan, how much you are guaranteeing, and what being a guarantor means.
3. To agree, tick **I agree**, sign on the screen and confirm with your PIN.
4. To say no, choose **Decline**. You can give a reason if you like.

Being a guarantor means this: if the member doesn’t repay, your society can take the amount you guaranteed from you. Part of your savings may be locked until the loan is repaid. Only agree if you are happy with that.
