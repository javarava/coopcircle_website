1. Find what you are paying for: **Deposit** on a savings account, fund, loan
   or shares, or a due.
2. Enter the amount.
3. Check the **convenience fee** (a small fee for paying online) and the total
   you will pay.
4. Choose **Pay**. Paystack’s secure page opens.
5. Pay with your card or bank, then come back to Coop Member.

The payment goes straight into your society’s own bank account, and is added to
what you paid for. You get a receipt in your notifications.

> If you don’t see the pay buttons, your society doesn’t take payments in the
> app yet. Pay at your branch as usual.
