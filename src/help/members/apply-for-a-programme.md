1. Go to **Accounts → Empowerment programmes**, or tap a programme on **Home**.
2. Open a programme. **Who can apply** shows each rule, and if you meet it.
3. Choose **Apply**.
4. Enter how much you are asking for (up to the most allowed) and what it is for.
5. Choose how you want to be paid: **into my savings**, **to my bank account** or **in cash**.
6. Add any documents that help, such as a price quote. Then choose **Send application**.

Your society’s president decides. You are told when it is approved, and when it is paid. Until it is paid, you can change how you want to be paid, or **Withdraw** it.
