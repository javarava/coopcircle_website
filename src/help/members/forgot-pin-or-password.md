## Forgot your password

1. On the sign-in screen, choose **Forgot password**.
2. Enter your email, or your phone number to get a text message.
3. Enter the 6-digit code.
4. Choose a new password. You are signed out on your other phones and computers.

## Forgot your PIN, or it’s locked

Your PIN locks after 5 wrong tries. Choose **Forgot PIN** (or go to **Profile → Account PIN**), enter your password, and set a new PIN.
