title: "Ask to withdraw from your savings"
description: "Ask to withdraw money from your savings in the app. Your society approves it before it is paid."
category: "accounts"
order: 2
app: "Coop Member"
popular: true
---
1. Go to **Accounts** and open your savings account.
2. Choose **Request a withdrawal**.
3. Enter the amount (up to what is available). You can say what it is for, if you like.
4. Confirm with your PIN.

Your society’s leaders approve it, then pay it, usually into [your bank account](/help/members/add-your-bank-account/). You are told at each step. You can **Cancel** the request until it is approved.

You can ask to withdraw from a fund the same way, if your society allows withdrawals from it.
