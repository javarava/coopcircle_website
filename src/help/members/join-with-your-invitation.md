Your society invites you after you are registered as a member. The invitation comes by **text message** or **email**, with a code.

1. Go to [member.coopcirclehq.com](https://member.coopcirclehq.com) (the Android and iPhone apps are coming soon).
2. Choose **Accept member invitation**.
3. Enter the email or phone number the invitation was sent to, and the code.
4. Create your account with a password, or sign in if you already have a CoopCircle account.

Your savings, dues, loans and shares are there at once.

> **No invitation?** Ask your society’s staff to send one from your member page. If your email changed, they need to update it first.
