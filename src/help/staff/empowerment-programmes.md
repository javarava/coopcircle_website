app: "Coop Staff"
---
1. Go to **Funds → Empowerment** and choose **New programme**.
2. Set the name, description and what members get. Set the **budget**, and the most each member can ask for.
3. Set the application dates and **who can apply**: months in the society, savings, credit score (Growth and up) and the number of places. If it isn’t for everyone, choose the branches.
4. Save it as a draft, then **Open** it.

Members apply in Coop Member and say how they want to be paid. Staff can also choose **Apply for a member**.

Only the president approves or rejects applications. Then choose **Record payment to member** for each one (cash, transfer or into savings), with your PIN. The programme’s page shows the budget, what is approved and what is paid. The member is told at every step.
