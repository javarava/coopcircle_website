---
title: "Approve and pay a savings withdrawal"
description: "Approve a member’s request to withdraw savings, then record the payment to them with your PIN."
category: "savings"
order: 3
app: "Coop Staff"
---
Members ask to withdraw in Coop Member. Staff can also ask for them from the member’s **Savings** card (**Withdraw**).

1. Go to **Funds → Savings withdrawals**. Open requests are listed first.
2. Open a request and choose **Approve**, or **Reject** with a reason. Approving needs your PIN.
3. When it has been paid (after any waiting period), choose **Record payment to member**. Add the reference or proof of payment. This needs your PIN.

You see the member’s confirmed bank account when you pay. The member is told at each step.

> A member’s new bank account can only be paid into after a leader approves it. See [Approve a member’s bank account](/help/staff/approve-bank-accounts/).
