Every society starts on **Free**. The president manages the plan:

1. Go to **More → Subscription** and choose **Compare plans**.
2. Choose **monthly** or **yearly** (if you pay for a year, you get two months free).
3. Choose a plan and pay by card through Paystack.

You see the payment, your new plan, the date it renews and your new limits. In **More → Subscription**, the president can also change the card, change the plan or cancel.

See [Pricing](/pricing/) for what each plan includes.
