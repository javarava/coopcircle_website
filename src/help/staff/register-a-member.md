title: "Register a member"
description: "Add a new member with their details, savings account and shares, and invite them to Coop Member."
category: "members"
order: 1
app: "Coop Staff"
popular: true
---
1. Go to **Members** and choose **Add member**.
2. Choose their **branch** and the date they joined. Leave **member number** empty, and CoopCircle gives them one.
3. Fill in their personal details. **Date of birth** is required.
4. Add their contact details, address, work and next of kin.
5. If you like, add their **bank account**. We check with the bank that it is in the member’s name. Then withdrawals and loans can be paid into it.
6. Choose their **savings account** (if your society has more than one savings type) and their **shares**, if they have any.
7. Save.

If you entered an email address, the member is sent an invitation to Coop Member. You can also [invite them later](/help/staff/invite-members-to-coop-member/).

> **A member who is joining again?** If they bring a CoopCircle file from another society, choose **Fill in from a CoopCircle file**. The form fills in from the file.
