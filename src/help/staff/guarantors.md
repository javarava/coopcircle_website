---
title: "How guarantors work"
description: "How guarantors agree to a loan: members agree in Coop Member or at the branch, and others sign your paper form."
category: "loans"
order: 3
app: "Coop Staff"
---
- **Members** named as guarantors get a request in Coop Member. They read about the loan and what being a guarantor means. Then they sign on the screen, or say no and give a reason.
- **At the branch,** staff can add a guarantor’s signed form to the application.
- **Guarantors who aren’t members** sign your society’s guarantor form. Staff upload it with the application, and your society confirms it.

The application shows each guarantor’s answer. If a guarantor signed in Coop Member, you can see their signature. If one said no, you can see their reason.
