The cash you collect is your **cash at hand**. Home and Funds show it, by what each payment was for.

1. Go to **Funds → Remittances** and choose **New remittance**.
2. Enter the date, how you paid it in, the amount, and the bank reference or a photo of the deposit slip.
3. The remittance includes all your cash at hand. Take out anything you aren’t handing in yet.
4. Add any [expenses](/help/staff/record-an-expense/) you paid from the cash.
5. Send it.

A leader approves it when the money arrives, or sends it back with a note. When it is approved, the payments in it count for the members.
