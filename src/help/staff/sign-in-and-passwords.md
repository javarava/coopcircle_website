Go to [staff.coopcirclehq.com](https://staff.coopcirclehq.com) and choose **Sign in**. Use your **email** or your **phone number** (once it’s confirmed on your account), and your password.

## Forgot your password?

1. On the sign-in screen, choose **Forgot password**.
2. Enter your email, or your phone to get a text.
3. Enter the 6-digit code you receive.
4. Choose a new password. You are signed out on every other phone or computer.

To change your password while signed in, go to **More → Change password**.
