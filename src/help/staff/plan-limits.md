title: "Plan limits"
description: "What happens when you get close to your plan’s limit for members, staff or branches."
category: "billing"
order: 2
app: "Coop Staff"
---
Each plan has limits on **active members**, **staff accounts** (invitations count) and **branches**, and on some features.

- **More** shows how much of each limit you use. A bar turns orange above 90%, and **Upgrade** appears.
- When you reach a limit, CoopCircle asks you to move to a bigger plan before you add more. Nothing you have recorded is lost.
- A feature your plan doesn’t include shows a lock and the plan that has it.

If a paid plan ends, your society moves to Free. Your records stay, and the president can [download all your data](/help/staff/download-all-your-data/).
