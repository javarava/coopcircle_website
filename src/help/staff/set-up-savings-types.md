title: "Set up savings types"
description: "Create the kinds of savings your society offers, such as regular and target savings, with their interest and rules."
category: "savings"
order: 1
plan: "Free: one savings type. Starter and up: unlimited"
app: "Coop Staff"
---
1. Go to **Funds → Savings** and choose **New savings type**.
2. Choose **Regular** or **Target**.
3. Set the **interest rate**, and how often interest is added.
4. Choose **how often members can save**: daily, weekly, monthly and so on.
5. For target savings, set the goal amount and date. Choose if members can set their own goals, and if they can’t withdraw until they reach the goal.
6. Choose **who opens accounts**: every member automatically, staff on request, or members themselves in Coop Member.
7. Save.

Every member has an account in your main savings type from the day they join.
