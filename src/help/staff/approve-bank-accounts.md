Every new or changed bank account waits for a leader: a member’s own, one staff add when registering a member, and the society’s. Until a leader approves it, withdrawals and loans are paid into the account already in use, if any.

Leaders are the president, financial secretaries and treasurers. You can’t approve a change you made yourself, unless you are the society’s only leader.

1. Go to **Home → Waiting for your approval → Bank accounts to approve**. You can also open the member and use their **Bank account** card.
2. Check the new account against the old one, and who saved it.
3. **Call the member** to make sure they made the change.
4. Choose **Approve** and enter your PIN. The account is used straight away.
5. Or choose **Reject**, say why, and enter your PIN. The member sees your reason.

The member is told by notification and email either way. If an account waits more than 2 days, the leaders are reminded, then again every week.

> **The society’s account** is approved the same way, in **More → Society settings → Member payments**, or from **Bank accounts to approve**.
