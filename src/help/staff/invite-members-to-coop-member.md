Members with an email address are invited when you register them. To invite anyone else, or to send an invitation again:

1. Open the member from **Members**.
2. In the **Coop Member** section, choose **Send invitation** (or **Resend**).
3. Choose to send it by **text message** or **email**.

The member opens the invitation, chooses **Accept member invitation** in Coop Member and creates their account. Their records are there at once.

The member’s page shows if they have been invited, if they have joined, and when the invitation ends.

> Text messages use your society’s SMS allowance. See **More → Plan and billing → SMS credit**.
