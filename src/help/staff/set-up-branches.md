Your head office is your first branch. Free, Starter and Growth have one branch. Professional allows up to 5, and Enterprise has no limit.

1. Go to **More → Branches** and choose **Add branch**.
2. Enter its name, a short code, address, state, phone and email.
3. Save it.

To give staff access to the new branch, open them in **More → Staff** and edit their branches.

## Closing a branch

You can’t delete a branch that has active members. First move its members to another branch (see [Move a member to another branch](/help/staff/move-a-member/)). Then choose **Turn off** on the branch’s page. You can turn it on again later.
