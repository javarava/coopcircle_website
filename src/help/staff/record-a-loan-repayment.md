---
title: "Record a loan repayment"
description: "Record a member’s loan repayment, so it shows on their repayment plan."
category: "loans"
order: 5
app: "Coop Staff"
---
Record a repayment like any other payment: go to **Funds → Record payment**, choose the member, then the **loan instalment**.

The repayment goes towards the next instalments. The loan’s page shows each instalment as paid, part paid or overdue (late). The member sees this in Coop Member.

Members can also repay online or by bank transfer in Coop Member, if your society takes payments in the app. **Loans → Repayments** lists every repayment.
