Members can apply themselves in Coop Member. To apply for them:

1. Go to **Loans → Applications** and choose **New application**, or choose **Apply for a loan** on the member’s page.
2. **The loan:** choose the member and the loan type. CoopCircle shows how much they can borrow, or why they can’t yet. Enter the amount and the **loan period** to see the cost and each instalment. Then enter what the loan is for.
3. **Guarantors and documents:** add the guarantors who are members, with how much each one guarantees. Add any guarantors who aren’t members, and the documents the loan type needs.
4. Submit.

If you add a guarantor’s signed agreement with the application, their guarantee counts at once. If not, they are asked in Coop Member.
