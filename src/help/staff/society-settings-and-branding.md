Everything about your society as a whole is in **More → Society settings**.

- **Society details:** the name, contact details and the title of your leader (President, Chairman, Chairperson and so on).
- **Branding:** your logo and your society’s colour. Coop Member shows them to your members.
- **Verification:** upload your registration certificate so CoopCircle can confirm your society is registered.
- **Share capital** and **Share register:** your share rules, and the list of who owns which shares.
- **Loan settings:** loan types and, on Professional and up, **Approval steps**.
- **Expenses:** expense categories, receipts and who approves (Starter and up).
- **Member payments:** your society’s bank account, and whether members can pay in the app (Starter and up).

Only the president can change most settings. Other staff can see them.
