Your society’s president (or chairman, or whoever leads it) creates the society. It starts on the **Free** plan, so you pay nothing to try it.

1. Go to [staff.coopcirclehq.com](https://staff.coopcirclehq.com/create-society) and choose **Create your society**.
2. **Society and contact:** the society’s name, registration details, address and contact details.
3. **Share capital:** the price of one share and your share rules, if your society has share capital. You can do this later.
4. **Head office:** your first branch. Every member belongs to a branch.
5. **Your membership:** your own details as a member of the society, including your date of birth.
6. **Your login:** your email, your phone number and a password.

When you finish, you are signed in as the president. Next, you can [invite your staff](/help/staff/invite-your-staff/) and [add branches](/help/staff/set-up-branches/).

> **Tip:** Add your logo and colour in **More → Society settings → Branding**, so Coop Staff and Coop Member look like your society.
