## Share capital

Set your share rules in **More → Society settings → Share capital**. Give a member shares when you register them, or later from their page. They pay over time, at the branch or in Coop Member.

**More → Share register** shows who owns which shares on any date. Download it as a PDF or a spreadsheet (CSV), or print it (paid plans). When a member has paid for their shares, you can issue a **share certificate** from their page.

## Dividends

The president or financial secretary goes to **More → Issue dividends**:

1. Enter the financial year, the record date (members who own shares on that day get a dividend), the amount to share out and a note.
2. Choose **Preview allocations** to see each member’s dividend. It is based on what each member has paid for their shares.
3. Confirm. Each dividend goes into the member’s savings, and they are told.
