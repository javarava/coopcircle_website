---
title: "Approve a loan and pay it to the member"
description: "Check a loan application, approve or reject it, then record the payment to the member with your PIN."
category: "loans"
order: 4
app: "Coop Staff"
---
1. Go to **Loans → Applications**. **To review** lists what’s waiting.
2. Open an application. You see the terms, **What they can borrow now**, the guarantors, the documents and which approvals are still needed.
3. Choose **Approve**, or **Reject** with a reason. You can also send it back to the member to change.
4. When it is approved, choose **Record the loan payment**. Enter how it was paid, the date and the reference. This needs your PIN.

The money goes to the member’s confirmed bank account, or to the vendor if the loan is paid to one. Nobody can pay their own loan to themselves.
