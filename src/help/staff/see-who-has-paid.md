---
title: "See who has paid and who is overdue"
description: "Find out who hasn’t paid their dues, in the whole society or in one branch."
category: "dues-funds"
order: 2
app: "Coop Staff"
---
- **Funds** lists each fund with who has paid this period, what is still to pay and what is **overdue** (late).
- Open a fund to see every member with their balance and what they owe.
- A member’s page shows what they owe in dues. Members see this in Coop Member too.
- The member statement (Starter and up) and the **Dues and funds** report (Growth and up) show the details for any dates.

On **Growth and up**, CoopCircle reminds members about dues automatically.
