You enter your PIN before you approve payments, remittances and withdrawals, mark cash as received, pay a member, or cancel a payment.

1. Go to **More → Account PIN**.
2. Enter your account password.
3. Choose a 4-digit PIN and enter it twice. Easy PINs like 1234 aren’t allowed.

If you don’t have a PIN yet, you’re asked to set one the first time you need it.

## Wrong tries

The PIN locks after **5 wrong tries**. Choose **Forgot PIN** and set a new one with your password.

## Hidden amounts

You can hide amounts on Home, Funds and Savings with the eye button. To show them again, enter your PIN. They hide again after a few minutes.
