1. Go to **Funds → Payment approvals**. The tile shows how many are waiting.
2. Open a payment. You see the member, what it is for, how it was paid, who recorded it, and the proof of payment.
3. Choose **Approve** (with your PIN), or **Reject** with a reason. The person who recorded it sees the reason.

You can’t approve a payment you recorded yourself.

## Cash not handed in yet

Below, you see cash and cheques that staff have taken but not handed in yet. Choose **Cash received** when the money reaches you. If you don’t, it counts when their remittance (cash hand-in) is approved.
