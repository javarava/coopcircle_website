---
title: "Close a member’s account"
description: "Close the account of a member who is leaving the society, once their money and loans are paid or cleared."
category: "members"
order: 4
app: "Coop Staff"
---
You can only close a member’s account when their loans are repaid, and their money has been paid to them or moved.

1. Open the member from **Members** and choose **Close account**.
2. CoopCircle shows anything still open: loans, savings, funds, shares and guarantees.
3. Clear each one. Then give the reason and confirm.

After that, the member only sees a message in Coop Member with your society’s contact details. Only staff can see the reason.

Members can also ask to close their own account in Coop Member. Your leaders then see the request and approve it.
