---
title: "Set up dues, levies and funds"
description: "Create the dues, levies and contribution funds your members pay into."
category: "dues-funds"
order: 1
plan: "Free: one contribution type. Starter and up: unlimited"
app: "Coop Staff"
---
1. Go to **Funds** and choose **New fund**.
2. Give it a name and choose its kind. For example: monthly dues, a one-off levy, or a fund members save into, such as welfare.
3. Set the **amount**, how often it is charged, and the deadline.
4. Choose if every member **must** pay it, and if members can **withdraw** from it (with a fee, if you charge one).
5. Save.

Dues every member must pay are charged on time. Each fund keeps its own balance, separate from savings.
