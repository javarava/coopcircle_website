---
title: "Print or share a member’s statement"
description: "Make a statement of a member’s savings, funds, shares and loans, for any dates."
category: "reports"
order: 2
plan: "Starter and up"
app: "Coop Staff"
---
1. Open the member and choose **Statement**. Or go to **More → Reports → Member statement** and choose the member.
2. Choose the dates.
3. Choose **Download** to share, download or print the PDF.

The statement shows every payment, with the balance at the start and at the end. Members can download the same statement themselves in Coop Member.
