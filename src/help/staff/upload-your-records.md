---
title: "Upload your records from Excel"
description: "Bring your members and their balances into CoopCircle from an Excel file."
category: "reports"
order: 3
plan: "Growth and up"
app: "Coop Staff"
---
1. Go to **More → Uploads from Excel**.
2. The president sets your **organisation start date**: the day your starting balances are counted from.
3. Choose **Download the blank Excel file**. It has a sheet for members, savings, share capital, dues and funds, and loans, with instructions and lists to choose from.
4. Fill it in and choose **Upload** (`.xlsx` or `.csv` files, up to 2,000 rows).
5. Fix any rows CoopCircle marks as a problem. Then send it for confirmation.

Another leader confirms the upload before it counts. You can **undo it for 7 days**. Then invite the new members to Coop Member.
