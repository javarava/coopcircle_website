When a member pays by bank transfer in Coop Member, they add the transfer reference, a screenshot, or both. It shows in **Funds → Payment approvals** as **Transfer claimed by the member**. The leaders who approve the branch’s payments are told.

1. Open it and look for the transfer in your society’s bank statement.
2. If it is there, **Approve** it. The money goes to what the member was paying for.
3. If it isn’t there, **Reject** it with a reason. The member is told it didn’t count.

The same reference or screenshot can’t be used twice. You can’t approve a transfer you sent yourself.
