---
title: "Record an expense"
description: "Record money the society spent, with the receipt, and send it for approval."
category: "cash"
order: 2
plan: "Starter and up"
app: "Coop Staff"
---
1. Go to **Funds → Expenses** and choose **New expense**.
2. Choose the branch (if you work at several) and what it was for.
3. Add the details, the amount and the day it was spent.
4. Say how it was paid: from cash at hand, from a fund, or with your own money (the society pays you back).
5. Add the receipt.
6. Send it for approval.

Leaders who approve expenses see it under **To approve**. Bigger amounts go to the president. You set this in **More → Society settings → Expenses**.
