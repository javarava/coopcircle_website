1. Go to **More → Reports**.
2. Choose a report. Reports your plan doesn’t include show a lock.
3. Choose the **period** (this month, last month, this year or your own dates) and the **branch**. For some reports, also choose the savings or loan type.
4. Read the summary, the chart and the tables on screen.
5. Choose **Download**: PDF, print or **Spreadsheet (CSV)**.

For long reports, CoopCircle suggests a spreadsheet (CSV). It opens faster in Excel or Google Sheets. Every download is recorded.

On **Professional and up**, choose **Scheduled** to have a report emailed to you regularly.
