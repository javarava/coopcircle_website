---
title: "Post a notice"
description: "Tell the society or one branch about meetings, rules and news, and see who has read it."
category: "more-features"
order: 3
app: "Coop Staff"
---
1. Go to **More → Notice board** and choose **New notice**.
2. Write the title and text.
3. Choose who it is for (**Everyone**, **Members** or **Staff**) and where (the whole society or one branch). You can’t change these after you post it.
4. Add a cover photo, up to 10 photos and any PDFs.
5. Choose **Publish now**, or set a time to post it.

Members see it in the Community tab of Coop Member. The notice shows how many people have read it, with likes and comments. Staff who manage notices can **Pin** a notice to keep it at the top, take it down or put it away.
