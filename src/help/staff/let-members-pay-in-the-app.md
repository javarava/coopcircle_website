When your society’s bank account is ready to use:

1. Go to **More → Society settings → Member payments**.
2. Turn on **Members can pay in the app** and confirm with your PIN. Only the president can change it.

Members then see **Deposit** buttons on their savings, dues, loans and shares.

- **Online payments** go through Paystack, straight into your society’s bank account. Members pay a small fee, and see it before they pay. You get the full amount.
- **Bank transfers** have no fee. They wait in **Payment approvals** until a leader checks them.

You can turn it off at any time. Payments already made are not affected.
