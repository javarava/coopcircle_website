1. Go to **More → Staff** and choose **Add**.
2. Enter their **name** and **email** (required), and their phone if you have it.
3. Choose their **role**: financial secretary, treasurer, secretary or branch admin. Add a title if your society uses one.
4. Choose their **branches**: one, several, or all of them.
5. If they are also a member of the society, turn on **Also a member of the society** and fill in their member details. They can then use the same login for Coop Staff and Coop Member.
6. Send the invitation.

They get an email with an invitation code. In Coop Staff, they choose **Accept staff invitation** and enter the code. Then they create their login, or sign in if they already have one.

## What each role can do

Each role comes with its own permissions (what that person is allowed to do). On **Growth and up**, the president can change them for each role in **More → What each role can do**, or for one person on their staff page.

> Invitations count towards your plan’s staff limit. When someone leaves, remove them in **More → Staff**.
