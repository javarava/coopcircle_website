---
title: "Verify your society"
description: "Upload your registration certificate so CoopCircle can confirm your society is registered."
category: "getting-started"
order: 5
app: "Coop Staff"
---
1. Go to **More → Society settings → Verification**.
2. Upload your society’s registration certificate as a photo or PDF.
3. CoopCircle checks it, and tells you the result.

Only the president can upload it. The certificate is kept private. Members can’t see it.
