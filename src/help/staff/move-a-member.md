---
title: "Move a member to another branch"
description: "Move a member to another branch. Their savings, loans and history move with them."
category: "members"
order: 3
app: "Coop Staff"
---
1. Open the member from **Members**.
2. Choose **Transfer**.
3. Choose the branch they are moving to, and give a reason.
4. Confirm.

Everything moves with them: savings, dues, loans and shares. The **Branch history** card on their page shows every move, with the reason and who made it.
