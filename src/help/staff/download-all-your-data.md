---
title: "Download all your society’s data"
description: "Your president can download everything about your society at any time, on every plan."
category: "reports"
order: 4
app: "Coop Staff"
---
1. Go to **More → Reports and records → Download all our data**. Only the president can.
2. The page explains what is in the download, then makes it.
3. When it’s ready, choose **Download**. The link is also emailed to you.

The download has your members, balances and records. It is on every plan, even Free, and even when your society’s use of CoopCircle is limited.
