---
title: "Cancel a payment"
description: "Cancel a payment that was recorded by mistake. We keep a record of it, but it no longer counts."
category: "payments"
order: 2
app: "Coop Staff"
---
Payments are never deleted. To fix a wrong one, cancel it:

1. Open the payment, for example from **Activities** or the member’s page.
2. Choose **Cancel payment**.
3. Give the reason and confirm with your PIN.

The cancelled payment shows on the member’s statement and in Activities, with who cancelled it and why. Then record the correct payment, if you need to.
