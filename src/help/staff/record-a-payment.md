title: "Record a payment"
description: "Record a savings deposit, dues, a loan repayment or a share payment from a member."
category: "savings"
order: 2
app: "Coop Staff"
popular: true
---
1. Go to **Funds** and choose **Record payment** (or open the member and record it from their page).
2. Choose the **member**.
3. Choose what they’re paying: savings, a due or levy, a loan instalment or shares.
4. Enter the **amount** and the **date** it was paid.
5. Choose **how** it was paid: cash, bank transfer, POS, cheque or salary deduction.
6. Add the **reference** or a **photo of the receipt**.
7. Save.

If your society checks payments, it waits in [Payment approvals](/help/staff/approve-payments/) until a leader approves it. Cash you take is added to your **cash at hand** until you [hand it in](/help/staff/hand-in-cash/).
