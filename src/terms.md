## 1. The service

CoopCircle is software that helps cooperative societies keep their records: members, savings, dues, loans, shares, payments and reports. **CoopCircle is not a bank, a lender, an insurer or a payment service.** We don’t hold your society’s or members’ money.

## 2. Societies and their staff

- The person who creates a society confirms that the society has allowed them to do so. The society is responsible for the people it invites as staff, and for what they do.
- The society decides its own savings and loan types, interest rates, rules and approvals. It is responsible for making sure these follow its bye-laws and the law.
- The society is responsible for making sure what it records is correct. It must also get any agreement it needs from its members to record their data. See our [privacy notice](/privacy/).

## 3. Members

Members use Coop Member to see their own records. If their society allows it, they can also make requests and payments. Questions about a member’s balance, or about a decision, are for their society to answer.

## 4. Accounts and security

Keep your password and PIN private. If you think someone else has used your account, tell your society or us straight away. You are responsible for what is done with your account until you tell us.

## 5. Payments

Paystack handles online payments, under Paystack’s own terms. The money goes into the society’s own bank account. Members pay a small fee when they pay online, and see it before they pay. Bank transfers go directly from one bank to another. CoopCircle isn’t responsible for delays or mistakes by banks or Paystack, but we will help find out what happened.

## 6. Plans and billing

- Societies start on the Free plan. You pay for other plans in advance, every month or every year, by card through Paystack. You pay the prices shown when you choose the plan.
- Plans have limits on members, staff, branches and features. We may change prices or plans. If we do, we will tell you before your next payment is due.
- If a society doesn’t pay for its plan again, it moves to the Free plan. Its records are kept, but it gets only the Free plan’s features and limits.
- We don’t refund fees already paid, unless the law requires it.

## 7. Acceptable use

Don’t use CoopCircle to break the law, to record false or dishonest payments, to harass anyone, to try to get into accounts or data that aren’t yours, or to stop CoopCircle working.

## 8. Your data

Your society owns its records. You can download all of your society’s data at any time, on every plan. Members can download their own. We use the data only to run CoopCircle, as our [privacy notice](/privacy/) explains.

We work to keep CoopCircle working and safe, but we can’t promise it will never stop or have errors. We improve CoopCircle over time. We may change or remove features, and we will tell you about big changes before they happen.

We may stop a society or account that breaks these terms. Where we reasonably can, we will warn you first. Before any data is deleted, we offer it for download.
