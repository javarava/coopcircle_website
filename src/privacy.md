This notice explains how {{ site.company.legalName }} (“CoopCircle”, “we”) handles personal data when you use our website, Coop Staff and Coop Member. We follow the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 (NDPA).

## Who is responsible for your data

CoopCircle plays two roles.

- **For your society’s records** (members, savings, loans, payments and so on), your **cooperative society is the data controller**. This means the society decides what it records and why. CoopCircle is its **data processor**. This means we keep and use those records for the society, only as it tells us to.
- **For accounts, the website and our own business** (your login, how you use the apps, contact form messages, billing), **CoopCircle is the data controller**. We decide what we record and why.

If you are a member with a question about your records, ask your society first. You can also contact us. If your request is for your society, we will pass it on to them.

## What we collect

- **Account details:** your name, email, phone number and password, and your PIN. We never store your password or PIN as you typed it. We only keep a scrambled version (a “hash”) that can’t be turned back into it.
- **Society records entered by staff:** members’ personal details (such as date of birth, address, next of kin, work and photo), membership, savings, dues, loans, guarantees, shares, insurance cover, programme applications and the documents attached to them.
- **Payment and bank details:** bank account numbers, and the account names the bank gives us when we check an account; payment references. You enter card details on Paystack’s page. They never reach CoopCircle.
- **Activity and security records:** who did what and when, sign-ins, approvals, and a history of changes.
- **Device and usage data:** basic information about your phone or computer and the app version, reports when the app stops working, and a code for your device so we can send you notifications.
- **Website data:** the pages people visit, and general information about their browser and country, counted without cookies; and what you send us through the contact form.

## How we use it, and why we are allowed to

| What we do | Why the law allows it |
|---|---|
| Run CoopCircle for your society: keep records, handle payments and approvals, send notifications | Our contract (agreement) with your society; and the society’s own reasons for keeping its records |
| Create and protect your account, check bank accounts, stop fraud and misuse | Our contract; and our fair business need (“legitimate interests”) to keep money and accounts safe |
| Charge societies for their plan | Our contract |
| Answer messages and requests for help | Our fair business need; and because you asked |
| Improve the apps and fix problems, using error reports and usage numbers | Our fair business need |
| Do what the law and regulators require, such as keeping financial records | The law requires it |

We don’t sell personal data, and we don’t use members’ records for advertising.

## Who we share it with

Only as needed to run CoopCircle, with companies that must protect it under their agreements with us:

- **Supabase**: our database, sign-in and server.
- **Cloudflare**: website hosting, private file storage, spam protection, and counting website visits without cookies.
- **Paystack**: online payments, bank account checks and charging societies for their plan.
- **Resend**: sending email (we plan to move to Amazon Web Services’ email service).
- **KudiSMS**: sending text messages, such as invitations and security codes.
- **Google Firebase**: notifications, error reports and app usage numbers.

In a society, the member can see their records. The society’s staff can see them too, depending on their role and branch. We may also share data when the law requires it.

## Where it’s stored

Some of these companies store or use data outside Nigeria. When they do, we use the protections that Nigeria’s data protection law (the Nigeria Data Protection Act, or NDPA) allows, such as written agreements to protect it.

## How long we keep it

We keep society records while the society uses CoopCircle, and for as long as the society and the law require. When a society closes for good, or a member’s account closes, we first offer the data for download. Then we delete it after the waiting time shown in the app. The law may require us to keep some records longer.

## Keeping it safe

Data is encrypted (scrambled so others can’t read it), both when it is sent and when it is stored. What each person can see depends on their role and branch. Important actions need a PIN, files are private, and changes are recorded. No system is completely safe, so please never share your password or PIN.

## Your rights

Under the NDPA you can ask to:

- see the personal data we hold about you, and get a copy;
- correct it, or have it deleted when the law allows;
- move it to another service (members can download their data in Coop Member, and societies in Coop Staff);
- say no to how it is used, or limit how it is used;
- take back your agreement, where we use your data because you agreed.

Members should usually ask their society first, because the society controls their records. To ask us, email [{{ site.company.privacyEmail }}](mailto:{{ site.company.privacyEmail }}). You can also complain to the **Nigeria Data Protection Commission**.

## Children

CoopCircle is for adults. Societies should only register children (under 18) when their bye-laws and the law allow it, and with a parent or guardian involved.

We will update this notice when what we do changes. We will tell societies about important changes.
