## This website

This website doesn’t use advertising or tracking cookies, so there’s no cookie banner.

- **Analytics:** we use Cloudflare Web Analytics, which counts page visits **without cookies** and without identifying you.
- **Spam protection:** the contact form uses Cloudflare Turnstile to check you are a real person, not a computer program. It may store what it needs for that check, only on the contact page.

## Coop Staff and Coop Member

The apps store what they need to work on your device:

- your sign-in session, so you stay signed in;
- the society you used last, and settings such as dark mode;
- on phones, a code for your device, so we can send you notifications.

These are necessary for the apps to work. Signing out removes your session.

## Changes

If we ever add cookies that aren’t necessary, we’ll ask for your consent first and update this page.
